Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Data controller: Xynix

This policy covers the LynkShare website and the LynkShare Android app (package app.xynix.lynk). LynkShare has no accounts, so there is no profile to create, sell or delete. The service is designed to move encrypted files without receiving the file key.

1. Summary

  • No account, name, email address or phone number is required.
  • Files are encrypted on the sender's device before they leave it.
  • The file key stays in the link fragment and is not sent to the server.
  • Session metadata is short-lived and is deleted automatically.
  • Encrypted Cloud objects are deleted by the bucket lifecycle within 24 hours.
  • The Android app uses Google AdMob; ad data is handled by Google as described below.

2. What we do not collect

  • Account registrations, usernames, passwords, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • Contacts, photos or files except the files you explicitly choose to send.
  • Precise location data.
  • File contents in readable form or the file-decryption key.

3. Files you transfer

Files are encrypted on the sending device. When Cloud delivery is used, the encrypted bytes are stored in Cloudflare R2. When Direct is used, the encrypted bytes travel over the direct connection and can fall back to the encrypted Cloud copy if the connection fails.

A transfer can normally be downloaded for 10 minutes, or 30 minutes after an extension. Encrypted objects are removed by the bucket lifecycle within 24 hours, and cancelling a transfer removes its objects immediately. After the download window closes, the link no longer provides access.

4. Transfer metadata

To operate a transfer, the backend processes:

  • A random transfer or receiver-session identifier.
  • File names, sizes and content types for the files you choose.
  • Timestamps and session status needed for expiry and cancellation.
  • IP address and network information needed to serve the request and limit abuse.

Session metadata is stored in Redis with a time-to-live and is deleted automatically, normally within 10 to 30 minutes. It is not used to build advertising profiles. Remaining transfer data, including metadata, is deleted within 24 hours.

5. Data stored on your device

  • Recent transfer history: names, sizes, timestamps and local paths.
  • Appearance and onboarding preferences.
  • The website keeps recent activity in the current tab's session storage.

Web history never stores the key, the complete link or the wrapped key. App history can be cleared from the app, and uninstalling removes local data.

6. Advertising

The Android app displays ads through Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, device characteristics, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. The app uses Google's consent tools where required and exposes privacy options when Google says they are required.

If ads are enabled on the website, they are served by Google AdSense. Google's handling of ad data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can limit ad personalization in your Google account or device settings; ads may still appear.

7. Permissions

  • Camera: used to scan QR codes. LynkShare does not take or store photos from the camera.
  • Internet and network state: required to transfer files and fetch session data.
  • File access: used when you choose files to send or save received files.

Permissions are requested when the related feature is used and can be revoked in device settings.

8. Third parties

  • Cloudflare R2 for encrypted Cloud objects.
  • Cloudflare and the hosting provider for network delivery and security.
  • A TURN relay when a direct connection needs one; it carries encrypted traffic and sees connection metadata, not plaintext.
  • Google AdMob and Google Play for the Android app.

9. Retention

  • Session metadata: normally 10 to 30 minutes; at most 24 hours.
  • Encrypted Cloud objects: within 24 hours, or immediately on cancel.
  • Local history and preferences: until you clear them or uninstall the app.
  • Server logs: retained only as needed for security, abuse prevention and legal obligations.

10. Your choices

  • Do not share a complete link, QR image or code with people you do not trust.
  • Clear recent activity in the app or the website tab.
  • Use the app's ad privacy options when required.
  • Ask the sender for a new link after a session expires.

11. Children

LynkShare is not directed at children under 13, and the service does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because there are no accounts, there is no child profile to maintain.

12. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page will be updated. For privacy questions, contact dev.muditp@gmail.com. For security reports, use the security policy.
