Troubleshooting

Expired or missing link.

LynkShare links are short-lived by design. A link can also look broken when its key is missing. The fix is usually the same: open the original link or ask the sender for a fresh one.

“This link is missing its key”

The file key lives in the part of the link after the # symbol. Browsers do not send that fragment to the server, and LynkShare does not store it. If the fragment was lost by copying only part of the URL, opening the page from browser history, or reloading a page after the fragment was scrubbed, the site cannot recover the key.

Open the original complete link. If you do not have it, ask the sender to share it again.

“This transfer has expired”

A transfer normally lasts 10 minutes. The Android app can extend the session to 30 minutes. When the session ends, the link no longer provides access, even if encrypted objects have not yet been cleaned up by the storage lifecycle.

Ask the sender to create a new transfer. A new link gets a new key and a new session; the old link cannot be revived.

Why a reload needs the original link

The key is deliberately not written to local storage or a session store. That protects it from later scripts and from a browser restart, but it also means a reload cannot restore it. Keep the original link until the transfer is finished.

What not to do

  • Do not paste a complete link into a public issue or chat.
  • Do not upload the link to a URL shortener; the fragment is the key.
  • Do not retry a missing-key page repeatedly; it cannot reconstruct the key.
  • Do not assume the sender can recover a link they did not keep.

Cloud cleanup is not the same as session expiry

The download window and the storage cleanup are separate. A session may end after 10 or 30 minutes while the encrypted object waits for the bucket lifecycle, which removes it within 24 hours. Once the session has expired, the link no longer authorizes a download.
