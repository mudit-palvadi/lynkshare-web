Receiving

Receive files on iPhone or iPad without an app.

LynkShare has no iOS app. iPhone and iPad users receive files through the browser in Safari or Chrome. Nothing needs to be installed, and no account is required.

Steps

  1. Open the link the sender shared.
  2. Wait for the transfer details to appear.
  3. Tap Save file.
  4. Use the browser's download or Share sheet to save it to the Files app.

What about Direct?

Safari and iOS can be unreliable with direct data channels. LynkShare detects that situation and uses the encrypted Cloud copy instead. If you are sending to an iPhone or iPad, Cloud is the predictable choice.

The web limit

The website allows up to 10 MB total per transfer, with at most 10 files. The Android app has higher limits, but the receiving device still needs a way to open the transfer. For an iPhone or iPad, the web receiver is the available path.

Saving to Files

The browser hands the file to iOS; where it lands depends on your Safari/Chrome download settings. Use the Share sheet and choose Save to Files if you want a specific folder. LynkShare cannot confirm that iOS wrote the file to disk; it can only confirm that the browser accepted the download.

Treat the link as sensitive

The complete link contains the capability to decrypt the transfer. Do not post it in a public chat or issue. If the session expires or the link is missing its key, ask the sender for a fresh one.
