Sending

Send files across devices without an account.

LynkShare is not limited to one kind of device. Use it between a phone and a laptop, two laptops, two phones, or a tablet and a computer. The sender can use the Android app or the web sender; the receiver can open a link in a browser or use the Android app.

There is no iOS app. iPhone and iPad users receive through the browser in Safari or Chrome. The web limit is 10 MB total per transfer.

What you need

  • A sender with files and either the Android app or a browser.
  • A receiver with a browser, or the Android app.
  • An internet connection. Direct also needs both devices online at the same time.

Steps

  1. On the sender, open LynkShare and choose the files.
  2. Choose Cloud if the recipient may open the link later. Choose Direct if both devices are online and you want the shortest path.
  3. Share the link or show the sender QR. If the receiver is showing a receiving QR, scan it from the sender instead.
  4. On the receiver, open the link, scan the sender QR, or show the receiving QR for the sender to scan.
  5. Save the file when the transfer finishes.

If the recipient does not have the app

Send a link. The receiver opens it in a browser, decrypts the file and saves it. No install is required on the receiving side. The web limit still applies, so a large file may need the Android app on the sending side and a compatible receiver.

What happens when Direct fails

Direct is an attempt, not a guarantee. Firewalls, carrier NAT, Safari limitations and restrictive networks can block it. When that happens, LynkShare can continue with the encrypted Cloud copy instead of asking you to start over.
