FAQ

Questions people ask before sending.

What is LynkShare?

LynkShare sends a file from one device to another without an account. The sender picks files, chooses Cloud or Direct, and shares a link or QR code. The receiver opens the link or scans the code, then saves the file.

Do I need an account or an app?

No account is required. The Android app is optional; the receiver can use the browser. The sender can also use the web sender. iPhone and iPad users use the browser because there is no iOS app.

Which devices does it work on?

Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, Chromebooks, iPhones, iPads and other devices with a modern browser. Android users can install the app; everyone else can use the web. There is no iOS app.

How do I send a file?

Pick your files, choose Cloud or Direct, then share the link or QR code. For a remote recipient, send the link. For someone in the same room, let them scan your sender QR.

How do I receive a file?

Open the link the sender shared, or scan the sender's QR with a camera. If the sender asks you to show a receiving QR, display it on your screen and let them scan it.

What is the difference between the sender QR and the receiver QR?

A sender QR is shown by the person sending; the receiver scans it or opens the link. A receiver QR is shown by the person receiving; the sender scans it to send to that waiting device. Use sender QR for remote links and normal sending, and receiver QR for an in-person “send to this device” flow.

Cloud vs Direct: which should I use?

Use Cloud when the recipient may open the link later, or when a direct connection is blocked. Use Direct when both devices are online and you want the shortest path. If Direct fails, LynkShare can continue with the encrypted Cloud copy.

Do both devices need to be online?

For Direct, yes. Both devices must be online at the same time. For Cloud, the sender uploads first and the recipient can open the link later while the session is active.

Can I send to more than one person?

A Cloud link can be opened by more than one recipient while the session is active. A Direct transfer is one-to-one.

What are the file and size limits?

The web limit is 10 MB total per transfer. The Android app allows 15 MB Cloud and 20 MB Direct on the standard tier, or 30 MB Cloud and 50 MB Direct after the in-app unlock. A transfer can contain up to 10 files, and the service ceiling is 50 MB.

How long does a transfer last?

A transfer normally lasts 10 minutes. The Android app can extend it to 30 minutes. After it expires, ask the sender for a fresh link.

Can I send multiple files or folders?

You can select up to 10 files in one transfer. Folders are not supported; choose the files inside the folder instead.

Why is my link missing its key or expired?

The file key lives in the original link and is not stored by the site. A reload needs the original link. If the session has expired, ask the sender for a new link. Do not paste a complete link into a public page.

Is my file encrypted?

Yes. Files are encrypted on the sending device before they leave it. The file key stays in the link and is not sent to the server. Cloud storage receives ciphertext only.

Can LynkShare see my files?

LynkShare cannot read your file contents. It stores ciphertext and session metadata such as file names, sizes and network addresses. Session metadata expires with the transfer, normally within 10 to 30 minutes, and all remaining transfer data, including metadata, is deleted within 24 hours.

Is LynkShare free?

Yes — LynkShare is completely free. There is no account, subscription or payment. The Android app contains ads to handle infrastructure costs, but the service itself is free to use.

LynkShare is open source. The Android app and website are released under GPL-3.0.
